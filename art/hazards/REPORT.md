## One problem in the assembly rule (game side, not art)

The brief says that for H < 128 the game draws only the `tip`, cut at the floor.

The tip has only 80 lethal rows (16–95). So for **H from 81 to 127**, the strip between row 96 and the floor is empty. It is lethal but has no art. The previews follow the brief's rule exactly, so the gap shows at H = 100 in every column preview, inside the red guide.

Suggested rule for the game:
- **H ≤ 80:** draw only the tip, cut at the floor.
- **80 < H < 128:** draw the tip, then draw the `base` last over the bottom 48 rows. The base collar covers the cut, so it is seamless.
- **H ≥ 128:** as specified.

### pipes: finished

Three dark iron pipes side by side, with flanges at staggered rows, a magenta rim on the left edges and a narrow highlight. The free end is staggered elbows: the middle one bends right and ends in a bolted flange face. The middle pipe has the cyan strip, and the side pipes have cyan rings under their flanges. Critic findings, checked:

### tesla: finished

The thin pedestal is widened into a faceted violet pylon with a teal edge light (as in the source) and coil windings every 16 rows. It has a recessed centre panel, and the source's arched panel sits in the flared foot.

The free end is chamfered shoulders and an emitter dome with a pulsing pink-white core. One vertical lightning trunk rises from it with upward forks, all inside x 14–129 and rows 0–35, in a different shape each frame. A dithered magenta glow surrounds the emitter. A glow pulse climbs the panel 16 rows per frame and lights the windings it passes.

Critic findings, all fair:
- The first lightning was a flat crown of twigs.
- There was no glow.
- Rows 16–43 beside the cap were mostly empty.

Fixed: redrew the lightning as a trunk with forks; added the stepped dithered aura, which raised the taper-zone minimum from 22 to 30 px; made the pulse a band that lights the windings; brightened the lit face and its cyan edge.

The aura dither first failed `validate` with 548–620 isolated stray pixels per frame. I fixed it with 2x2 clustered dither plus a cleanup pass that removes any fx pixel with no 4-neighbour.

**Likeness: moderate.** The colours, teal edge, arched panel and pink-white lightning carry over. The lightning is necessarily much shorter relative to the body than in the source, because it can only live in rows 0–43 of the tip. ### crystal: finished

Built from `obstacles_05_cristal.png` only. **`obstacles_10_cristal_grande.png` is not a crystal:** it is a long magenta energy-conduit machine, so I used it only as a colour check.

Four faceted prisms packed shoulder to shoulder. Each has lavender lit faces, a violet middle and an indigo shadow face, and a soft cyan core segment that pulses. A diagonal glint sweeps across the lit facets, 16 rows per frame, and a few sparks sit in the overhang rows. The free end is ragged, asymmetric points: one dominant centre point with leaning side apexes and uneven shoulders. The foot is a dark slate ledge (the collar) over slab stones with small shards.

Critic findings:
- Pyramid tips on parallel prisms read as crayons or pipes rather than the source spire.
- The colours were too saturated and magenta.
- The cores had dark ends and a crossing line, so they read as electronic parts.

Fixed: ragged asymmetric points; lavender over an indigo palette; soft cores of varied length (12, 20, 16 and 10 rows) with no dark caps; diagonal facet steps on the lit side only; a cool slate foot with less flare.

Not fixable under the rules: the critic asked for one point 50–60 rows tall. The fairness rule only allows tapering in the first 28 lethal rows, so every point must reach full width by row 43. The points stay fairly short.

**Likeness: moderate to weak.** The colours and the crystal read are right, but a cluster that fills a 120 px column will always look more like a palisade than the source's single spire.

### drone: finished

A tall capsule, by necessity, since the middle repeats:
- **Rotor:** two thick blades with magenta tips on even frames, a blur disc on odd frames, and a hub, all inside x 12–131 in the overhang rows.
- **Body:** dark slate with a curving violet rim, shaded from 3D normals with a light clustered dither between steps. The cyan band flickers slightly, and there is one band per 48-row tile.
- **Eye:** a large grey eye pod on the front with a pulsing magenta lens.

Critic findings:
- The 1–2 px rotor read as an antenna, so the whole thing read as a canister.
- The vertical stripe shading turned it into a cylinder.

During the fix the larger eye pod spilled into rows 98–99 of `mid` and broke the join check. I caught it with `check.py` and clipped the pod to the bottom piece. The joins are identical again.

**Likeness: moderate.** The colours, rotor, cyan bands and magenta eye match. The squat saucer silhouette cannot survive the repeat, and the 60 px height is closest to the source.

## Tool notes and workarounds

- **`preflight`, `sprite_manage`, `palette load`, `layer`, `frame`, `tag`, slices, `draw image`, `export spritesheet` and `validate`** all worked without errors.
- **`draw` op `image`** accepts absolute paths, and it erases transparent pixels in its rectangle (full-canvas replacement).

- **`validate` counts single-pixel dither as stray errors.** I used 2x2 clustered dither and a cleanup pass.

- **The critic cannot zoom.** It got pre-upscaled images from `critic_prep.py`. One of its blocking claims was measurably false: the pipes notch. Its scores are rough.
